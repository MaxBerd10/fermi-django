# Script 75: Russian and English names of the 19 annexes (translations to be read by a native speaker)

The Uzbek names are the titles printed on the first page of each PDF (checked against the PDF text). The Russian and English names are my translations of
them; the Ministry's own Russian/English titles were not available. Terms used: siyosat = политика / policy, uslubiyot = методика / methodology,
nizom = положение / regulation, reglament = регламент / rules, yoʻriqnoma = инструкция / instruction, uslubiy qoʻllanma = методическое пособие /
methodological guide, chek-list = чек-лист / checklist.

Please check especially: ru and en of annexes 4, 9 and 10 (reglament), 12 (the long gift regulation), 13 (rewarding) and 14 (the antimonopoly compliance system).
