The Ministry of Higher and Secondary Specialised Education, having drawn up students' applications for transfer and reinstatement in accordance with the procedure set out in these Regulations, submits them to the Interdepartmental Commission for decision-making.
Transfer and reinstatement of students from non-state higher education institutions without a license operating in the territory of the Republic of Uzbekistan, branches of foreign higher education institutions (centres, departments, counseling centers, etc.) to higher education institutions of the Republic of Uzbekistan is prohibited.
Transfer of students and reinstatement to the first year of study is prohibited (as an exception, students for health reasons may be granted academic leave).
Transfer in all relevant and related areas (specialties) of all forms of study for training on the basis of a state grant is possible only on the application of students studying on the basis of a state grant and in the presence of places allocated for training on the basis of a state grant in a given academic year in a higher educational institution.
In all other cases, the transfer of students is carried out on a fee contract basis.
The student's application for transfer or reinstatement of studies is accompanied by the student's rating book or a certain standard academic certificate.

Министерство высшего и среднего специального образования, оформив заявления студентов о переводе и восстановлении на учебу, в определенном данным Положением порядке, представляет Межведомственной комиссии для принятия решения.
