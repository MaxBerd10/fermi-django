# Script 78: corrections of the Russian and English texts found by two independent reviewers

Two reviewers (a second model, working read-only) read the Russian and English of five pages against the Uzbek: the technical-college admission regulation,
the regulation on admission to universities (qabul-nizomi), the instruction for bachelor's applicants (bakalavriat-uchun-yoriqnoma) and four service pages (registrar,
accounting, youth work, employers' survey): 244 blocks. They reported 28 findings; each was checked against the Uzbek before it was taken over (25 taken over, 3 not).
Every list has the same number of items in uz, ru and en (checked by a script).

NOT taken over (to be decided by people who know the subject):
* registrator-ofisi #13, ru and en: "hisob varagʻini shakllantirib berish" - "лицевого счёта" / "personal account statement" may really be an invoice for payment
  ("счёт на оплату" / "invoice"). The accounting office should say.
* ish-beruvchilarning-institut-bitiruvchilari-xaqida-fikri #2, ru and en, item 0: "ishlab keta olish" is read as "keep working" now; it may mean "cope with the work".
* tibbiyot-texnikumi-biturvchilarini-qabul-qilish-nizomi #23, Uzbek: "... va boshda oliy taʼlim muassasasining ... professor-oʻqituvchisi" - "boshda" looks garbled
  (both translations say "as a rule"); it should be compared with the original decree.

Typos of the Uzbek source that WERE corrected: "hududlari" -> "huquqlari" (rights), "unversitetning", "3-ilovadabelgilangan", "Manbaa:".
Still open in the Uzbek source: "OTTda" (registrator-ofisi #17, item 3) - probably "OTMda", to be confirmed.

The link to HEMIS on the registrar page was wrong in all three languages: the text said hemis.fsjti.uz (a typo; the address does not exist) and in Uzbek the address
pointed to hemis.samdu.uz (the HEMIS of another university). It is https://hemis.fjsti.uz/ now (the address the rest of the site uses).
