# Script 62: what we wrote — please let uz / ru / en readers check it

These pages said different things in the three languages (different numbers of blocks, a language missing). The Uzbek text is the base; the Russian and English blocks were aligned to it by hand and where a language had no counterpart the text was written by us. Nothing else was changed except: Uzbek apostrophes (U+02BB), a few old spellings (x → h in Maxkamasi, xamkor, xozir, xolda, xaqida, hujjat, ...), CAPITAL headings turned into normal case, lists made from lines '1.' / '•' / '–'.

In this script every language already had its own text for every block: nothing was translated.
